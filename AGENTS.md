# Wakata project instructions

For design system governance, read `docs/ds/governance.md` before proposing, approving, or promoting shared components or extensions. The project-local Design System Ops v2.0.0 package is at `.claude/skills/design-system-ops/`; its skills are review aids, while Wakata's governance policy and actual repository gates determine decisions. Use `.agents/skills/wakata-ds-governance/SKILL.md` when preparing a Wakata contribution or promotion review.

Preserve existing component work. Do not treat a provisional fork or planned adoption as production usage. Do not claim that a written policy automatically verifies production use or reviewer identity. Do not push, publish, deploy, merge, or change credentials without specific authorization.

Implementation conventions and commands are in `.github/copilot-instructions.md` and `packages/ds/README.md`.
